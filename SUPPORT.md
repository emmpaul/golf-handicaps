# Support

This is a personal project, maintained in spare time. Answers are best effort, with no guaranteed delay.

## Where to ask

- **Something is broken.** Open a [bug report](https://github.com/emmpaul/golf-handicaps/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include the version from the page footer or from `/api/version`.
- **An idea or a missing feature.** Open a [feature request](https://github.com/emmpaul/golf-handicaps/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **A security problem.** Do not open an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Setting it up.** Read the [README](README.md) and the [docs](docs/) first, in particular [Deployment](docs/deployment.md) and [Development](docs/development.md). If they do not answer, open a bug report and say which step failed.

## What is out of scope

- Scorecards from simulators other than GSPro. The panel detection and OCR only know the GSPro layout.
- Translating the user interface. The app is in French, and the specs fix its wording.
- Help with your hosting platform beyond what [Deployment](docs/deployment.md) covers.
